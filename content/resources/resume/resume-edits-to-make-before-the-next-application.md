1. **Add a 2-line summary at the top.** Recruiters spend 7 seconds. Something like: *"Backend-focused full-stack engineer (3 yrs) who owns products end to end — built and operates an AI-powered assessment platform on EKS serving 60+ concurrent isolated environments, plus a live multi-tenant cloud SaaS. Strong in Node/Rails/Java, AWS, Kubernetes and production observability."*
2. **Tier the skills section** (Expert / Proficient / Familiar) and drop or demote anything you can't be grilled on.
3. **Fix the education/employment date overlap** or annotate it.
4. **Reframe the "20% flagged"** line so it doesn't read as a false-positive rate.
5. **Add the cost-optimization work explicitly** — "modelled and reduced infrastructure cost by X% via Graviton migration, Savings Plans and right-sizing." Cost ownership is rare at 3 YOE and reads as senior.
6. **Promote the leadership/scope signals**: number of engineers guided, code reviews owned, cross-team adoption (6 apps), stakeholder-facing work (client visit for field validation).
7. **Add "TypeScript"** if you can honestly claim it — the JD names it explicitly.
8. **Quantify CloudNap users** (accounts, instances managed, dollars saved) — a live product with real users is worth more than any bullet if you make the traction visible.
9. Keep it to two pages. It's currently dense; tighten wording rather than cutting substance.
