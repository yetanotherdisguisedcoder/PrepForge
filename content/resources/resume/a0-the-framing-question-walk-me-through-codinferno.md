**Prepare a 3-minute version and a 30-second version.** Structure:
> Problem (what eLitmus needed) → Constraints (multi-tenant, secure, cost, scale) → Architecture (5 services, what each does) → My specific ownership → Hardest problem → Outcome with numbers → What I'd change.

Draw the architecture from memory on a whiteboard in 90 seconds. Practise this.

**Landmine:** "Project Lead" — they will ask *how many people*. Answer precisely and honestly: who, for how long, what you delegated, how you reviewed. Don't inflate. "I owned the technical direction and was the sole/primary engineer on X, with N contributors on Y" is a strong, honest answer.
