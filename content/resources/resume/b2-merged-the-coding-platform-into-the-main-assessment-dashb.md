**They will ask:**
- This is a migration/consolidation project — how did you sequence it to avoid a big-bang cutover? (Strangler fig? Feature flags? Parallel run?)
- How did you handle two different auth systems / session models?
- Data migration: how did you merge the two data models? Conflicts?
- How did you avoid regressions for existing clients?
- Who pushed back and how did you get alignment? (**Great leadership story — cross-team, cross-product.**)
- How long did it take vs your estimate? What did you underestimate?
- Rollback plan if clients hated it?

**This bullet is under-sold on your resume.** Consolidating two products into one login is an architecture + stakeholder + migration story all at once. Rehearse it as a lead-behaviour example.
