- **Clarify before answering.** Every design question, every ambiguous behavioral question.
- **Think out loud.** Silence is scored as being stuck.
- **Put a number in every story.**
- **Volunteer one trade-off or failure mode in every technical answer.** This single habit is the clearest senior signal you can send.
- If you don't know something: *"I haven't worked with that directly. Here's how I'd reason about it, and here's the closest thing I have shipped."* Never bluff. Never spiral.
- Take notes. It reads as engagement and gives you material for follow-up.
