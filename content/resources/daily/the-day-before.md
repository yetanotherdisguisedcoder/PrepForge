- Re-read only two things: your **Numbers Sheet** and your **12 stories**. No new topics.
- Confirm logistics: address, time, who you're meeting, format, what to bring.
- Look up your interviewers on LinkedIn — what they've built tells you what they'll ask.
- Prepare 3 questions per round, written down.
- Test your setup if remote: camera, mic, whiteboard tool (Excalidraw), backup internet.
- Print or open your resume. **Interviewers ask about the resume in front of them, not the one in your head.**
- Stop by 9pm. Sleep is a performance input.
