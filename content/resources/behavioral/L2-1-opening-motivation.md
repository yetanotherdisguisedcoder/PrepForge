1. Tell me about yourself. *(90 seconds. Scripted. Present → past → why here.)*
2. Walk me through your resume.
3. **Why are you leaving eLitmus?** *(Forward-looking only: scope, scale, team, learning. Never bitterness. Practise this until it's boring.)*
4. Why this role / this company? What do you know about us?
5. Why do you want a Tech Lead role now, at 3 years?
6. What are you looking for that you don't have today?
7. What's your ideal team and working environment?
8. Where do you see yourself in 3 years? 5 years?
9. What kind of work energizes you? What drains you?
10. What are you currently learning outside of work?
11. Why should we hire you over someone with 6 years of experience? *(Answer: ownership density, not years. You've shipped a product, run its infra, and built a side SaaS.)*
12. What questions do you have for me?
