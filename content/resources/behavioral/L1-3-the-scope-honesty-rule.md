You are a **project lead**, not a people manager. Don't pretend otherwise — experienced interviewers detect it instantly. Instead, use precise scope language:

> "I haven't managed direct reports. What I've owned is technical direction for CodInferno end to end — architecture decisions, code review, breaking work down for contributors, and being the person accountable when it breaks at 2am. The leadership muscle I've built is influence without authority: getting six other teams to adopt a shared gem, or convincing my manager to fund a Ruby 3 migration."

This answer is *stronger* than a fabricated management story, because it's specific and verifiable.
