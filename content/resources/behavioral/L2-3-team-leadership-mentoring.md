37. What's your philosophy on pair programming?

39. How much of your time should be hands-on coding as a lead? *(A defensible answer: 30–50%, and I protect team throughput over my own output.)*
40. How do you handle being the bottleneck (everything routes through you)?
41. Have you interviewed or hired anyone? What do you look for?
42. How do you handle on-call fairness and burnout?
42a. **A strong performer on your team wants to resign — walk me through that conversation.** *(Common Indian tech-lead archetype — distinct from the underperformance/feedback questions above. Cover: understanding the real reason before reacting, when a counter-offer is/isn't appropriate, protecting the transition and knowledge handoff either way.)*
42b. **How do you write a performance review or calibrate a rating for someone on your team?** *(Distinct from giving live feedback — this is about the formal, backward-looking, comparative process: evidence-gathering, avoiding recency bias, calibrating against peers.)*
42c. **Tell me about a time you built the case for promoting someone you don't formally manage.** *(Sits next to the mentoring questions — shows you advocate for people's growth beyond your own reporting line, relevant since you were a project lead, not a formal manager.)*
