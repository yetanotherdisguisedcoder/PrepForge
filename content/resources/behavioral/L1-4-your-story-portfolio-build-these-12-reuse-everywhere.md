Fill each in fully before your first interview. Most questions are one of these twelve wearing a different hat.

| # | Story | Primary use | Backup uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **EKS Fargate architecture + load-test bottleneck** | Most complex technical problem | Scale, trade-offs, data-driven decisions |
| 2 | **SSL renewal pipeline failure across dallas** (stale creds + missing Route53 IAM + broken Chef LWRP) | Hardest debugging | Incident response, persistence, systems thinking |
| 3 | **Credits System** (20+ accounts, billing automation) | Business impact | Correctness under concurrency, stakeholder work |
| 4 | **Dashboard consolidation** (two products → one) | Cross-team influence | Migration strategy, managing resistance |
| 5 | **Rails 7 / Ruby 3 migration** | Selling tech debt to the business | Risk management, sequencing |
| 6 | **Health-check gem adopted by 6 apps** | Influence without authority | Building for other engineers, internal tooling |
| 7 | **Prometheus/Grafana observability build-out** | Proactive ownership | Reliability, MTTD, on-call improvement |
| 8 | **CloudNap end-to-end** (side project → live SaaS) | Initiative & ownership | Product sense, cloud security design |
| 9 | **Assessment kit / rubric design + AI-usage criterion** | Judgment & design thinking | Fairness, measurement validity, product |
| 10 | **Cost optimization model** (Graviton, Savings Plans, Fargate quirks) | Business/commercial thinking | Data-driven persuasion |
| 11 | **Field validation visit to a partner company** | Customer empathy | Handling ambiguity, feedback loops |
| 12 | **A time you were wrong / a deploy that broke** | Failure & learning | Humility, blameless culture |

⚠️ **You need story #12 and you must not fake it.** Every strong interviewer asks for a failure. Pick a real one, own the decision (not circumstances), and lead with what changed permanently as a result.
