| Beat | What goes in it | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ituation | Context + stakes. Why did this matter to the business? | 15s |
| **T**ask | *Your* specific responsibility. Say "I", not "we". | 10s |
| **A**ction | 3–4 concrete steps, including what you *considered and rejected* | 60s |
| **R**esult | A number. Always a number. | 15s |
| **L**earning | What you'd do differently / what you changed permanently | 20s |

**Total: ~2 minutes.** Anything over 3 minutes loses the room. Practise with a timer.
