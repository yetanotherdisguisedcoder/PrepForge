Pick a real one with visible mitigation. Two that fit you honestly:
- *"I default to building rather than delegating — it's faster in the moment and it's cost me team growth. On CodInferno I started deliberately handing off components even when it slowed me down, with review as the safety net rather than doing it myself."*
- *"I've validated late. On the EKS work I built the whole provisioning layer before load testing it. Now I front-load the measurement — I'd rather find the bottleneck at 20% built than 100%."*

Both are true, both are technical rather than fake-humble, and both come with a behaviour change.
