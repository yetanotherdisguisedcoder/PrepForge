Prepare three per round, tailored:
- What does the first 90 days look like, and what would "clearly working out" look like at six months?
- How big is the team, what is the seniority mix, and who would I be leading versus peering with?
- New team/product, or taking over something existing? What is the state of the codebase and tech debt?
- What is the current deployment frequency, and how long from merge to production?
- How do you handle on-call and incidents today?
- What is the split between hands-on coding and leadership expected in this role?
- Who makes architecture decisions today, and how are disagreements resolved?
- What is the biggest technical risk the team is carrying right now?
- What made the last person in this role successful — or why is it open?
- How does the company decide what to build? How close is engineering to customers?
- What does progression look like from Tech Lead here?
