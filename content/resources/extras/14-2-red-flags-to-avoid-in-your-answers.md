- Saying "we" for everything. Interviewers need **your** contribution: "the team did X; I specifically owned Y."
- Claiming tools you cannot defend. **Kafka is the risk on your resume** — either study it this month or downgrade it to "exposure, not production depth."
- Blaming your employer or manager when asked why you are leaving.
- A ten-minute answer to a one-minute question.
- No numbers. Every story needs a metric: latency, cost, users, incidents, time saved.
- Not asking clarifying questions in design rounds.
- Presenting only the happy path. Always volunteer the failure mode.
- Overclaiming leadership. Be precise: "I led a three-person feature squad" beats a vague "I led the team."
- Saying "it just works" about something you did not build.
