- [ ] Know your current CTC breakdown cold (fixed / variable / ESOP / benefits)
- [ ] Have three numbers ready: target, walk-away, and a justification anchored on market plus scope
- [ ] The band is stated (₹15–20L). At 3 YOE with your infra plus product-ownership scope, anchoring in the top third is defensible — lead with **scope and evidence**, not need
- [ ] Deflect early salary questions: "I would like to understand the scope first. My expectation is in line with the band you have listed, and I am confident we can align."
- [ ] Notice period, joining date, WFO logistics — this role is office-based in Bengaluru; decide **before** the final round
- [ ] Get the offer in writing; compare fixed vs variable vs ESOP realistically
- [ ] Never accept on the call; ask for 48 hours
